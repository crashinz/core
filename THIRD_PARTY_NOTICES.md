## Blackjack CC0 sound effects

The selected normalized Blackjack sound files under `assets/audio/blackjack/`
are derivatives of these CC0 sources:

- **Casino Audio**, Kenney: <https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio> and
  <https://opengameart.org/content/54-casino-sound-effects-cards-dice-chips>
- **Playing Card Sounds**, Brian MacIntosh / BMacZero:
  <https://opengameart.org/content/playing-card-sounds>
- **Card Game sounds**, HaelDB:
  <https://opengameart.org/content/card-game-sounds>
- **Basic Sound Effects**, n4:
  <https://opengameart.org/content/basic-sound-effects>

CC0 permits copying, modification, and redistribution, including from a public
repository. CoreChat converted selected source files to normalized mono 44.1 kHz
MP3 derivatives. Exact per-file mapping is in `assets/audio/blackjack/README.md`.
No source author endorses CoreChat.
## Hearts public card-game sound effects

The Hearts files under `assets/audio/card-games/` are renamed copies of the
normalized CC0 derivatives documented in `assets/audio/blackjack/README.md`.
Their original sources are Kenney Casino Audio, BMacZero Playing Card Sounds,
HaelDB Card Game sounds, and n4 Basic Sound Effects. Exact per-file mapping is
in `assets/audio/card-games/README.md`. No source author endorses CoreChat.

## Chess Piece Slide and Piece Capture (CC0)

`assets/audio/built-in-games/chess-piece-slide.mp3` and
`assets/audio/built-in-games/chess-piece-capture.mp3` are renamed copies of
“Piece Slide” and “Piece Capture” by El Final Boss, recorded for Chess Puzzle
Blitz and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Source records:

- <https://freesound.org/s/546118/>
- <https://freesound.org/s/546120/>

The recordings are used only for the Built-in Chess presentation. Original OCX
audio remains private installation media and is not replaced or redistributed.
