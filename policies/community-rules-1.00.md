# Community Rules

Version 1.00

Treat other members with respect. Do not threaten, harass, impersonate, spam,
exploit, or publish content you do not have the right to share. Do not evade a
mute, block, restriction, suspension, room decision, or moderation action.

Use reporting for genuine safety concerns. Reports are reviewed by authorized
staff and never punish an account automatically. A material update requires
you to review and accept the complete current Community Rules before ordinary
access resumes.
